Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about file security, QR customizations, and developer tools.

Are my uploaded files secure?

Yes. Because ToolHub Pro executes basic actions (PDF Merging, Splitting, Rotating, and image transformations) locally inside your browser, the documents never leave your computer. For conversions that require remote helpers, files are processed in-memory and destroyed immediately after download.

How do Dynamic QR Codes work?

Dynamic QR Codes direct scanning users to our redirect portal, which counts the analytics parameters (device, referrer, geo-location, user-agent) before sending them to your actual configured link. This lets you edit the destination link at any time without changing the QR code graphics!

Can I automate these operations using an API?

Yes, Pro members can generate a secret API key via settings. You can review and test the Swagger/REST endpoints under our documentation tab to merge documents or render QR graphics programmatically.
